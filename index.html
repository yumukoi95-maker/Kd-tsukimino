⚙️ School Scheduling Rules (V10.0)
1. 13:50 Early Kids = Manual Only: 13:50 (Early Kids) Lead & Assist stay untouched/blank during Auto-Fill so you can set them manually.
2. Hallo (3x), Prime (18:00-19:30) & Bus Strictly Excluded: Any teacher in Hallo, Prime, or Bus during a time window will NOT be placed in Kindy or Elementary (neither Lead nor Assist).
3. Smart Auto-Assist: Teachers who are not leading (and not in Hallo/Prime/Bus) automatically assist! Elementary shows multiple teachers comma-separated (e.g. Anna, Mitchelle), and KINDY shows them in brackets (Anna).
4. Kindy Fixed Blocks & 17:05 Class 1: Kindy 16:15–17:05 and 17:00(ABC)–17:50 keep the same Lead teacher; Elementary Class 1 runs 17:05–17:50.
5. Manual Date/Day & Kato Shift Blank: Date/Day are preserved on Auto-Fill, and Director Kato's shift stays blank.
